\# 🌱 GreenChain



GreenChain is a full-stack agricultural marketplace designed for Nepal.



It connects local farmers directly with customers, helping farmers sell

their agricultural products while giving customers an easy online

shopping experience.



\## 🚀 Features



\### 👤 Customer

\- Registration and login

\- JWT authentication

\- Customer profile

\- Customer dashboard

\- Product marketplace

\- Search and filters

\- Product details

\- Shopping cart

\- Wishlist

\- Place orders

\- Cancel orders

\- Order tracking

\- Product reviews

\- Payment records



\### 👨‍🌾 Farmer

\- Farmer registration and login

\- Farmer dashboard

\- Add products

\- Update products

\- Delete products

\- Product image upload

\- Manage product quantity

\- View customer orders

\- Update order status



\### 👑 Admin

\- Admin dashboard

\- Manage users

\- Manage products

\- Manage orders

\- View payments
